What water leak detection covers and how it works

The work starts with a discussion of the affected area and any previous repairs. Assessment and water intrusion testing help investigate where moisture may be entering. Building envelope testing and moisture mapping can also support repair planning, while scheduled inspections can help managed properties keep track of exterior conditions.

Key facts to keep in mind:

  • Coverage: Investigations may relate to roofs, waterproofing, balconies, stairs, drainage, stucco, or protective coatings, depending on the concern.
  • Typical timing: Scheduling and evaluation time depend on the affected area, access, and the testing scope. Repair work may be planned separately.
  • What affects cost: The size and location of the area, access, testing needs, and the kind of repair being considered can affect the overall scope. Ask providers to explain what is included before comparing proposals.
  • Planning: Findings can help determine whether roof repairs or reroofing, waterproofing, drainage improvements, or other exterior work should be considered.

A few terms you may hear: water intrusion means water entering a building. Moisture mapping is a way of documenting where moisture is found. Building envelope refers to the exterior parts of a building that separate indoor space from outside conditions.
