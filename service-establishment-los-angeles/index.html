Scope, timing and terms to know

Key facts about the service:

  • It may cover assessment, water intrusion testing, moisture mapping, building envelope testing and scheduled inspections.
  • Repair work may include roof repair or reroofing, waterproofing, balcony and stair repairs, drainage improvements, stucco repair and protective coatings.
  • Timing depends on the area, the evaluation or testing needed, the repair scope and property coordination. Discuss scheduling after the affected area and access needs are understood.
  • Cost depends on factors such as the size and condition of the area, the testing requested, access and the work identified. Ask providers to describe what their proposed scope includes; no general figure fits every property.

A few terms may come up. “Water intrusion testing” means checking how water may enter a building assembly. “Moisture mapping” records where moisture is found. “Building envelope” refers to the parts that separate a building’s interior from the outdoors, including walls, roofs and other exterior components. These terms describe evaluation tools and building parts, not a guaranteed diagnosis or repair result.
