Start by finding the source

The best water leak detection is a careful investigation of how water may be getting into a building. A stain, damp area or damaged finish can point to a concern, but it may not identify the entry point. Exterior Systems Construction starts with an assessment and water intrusion testing to help understand the path of moisture before recommending repairs.

This approach can help homeowners, HOAs, property managers and commercial clients make decisions based on the affected area and the building system involved. It also gives you a chance to discuss earlier repairs and what has changed since they were completed.

Why bring in Exterior Systems Construction

Exterior Systems Construction works on exterior building systems as well as leak evaluation. If testing identifies a concern, the team can discuss relevant repair options such as roof work, waterproofing, balcony or stair repairs, drainage improvements, stucco repairs and protective coatings. The right work depends on the findings, so the evaluation comes before a recommendation.

When comparing providers, ask what areas they will assess, what testing they propose, and how they will explain the findings. A useful evaluation should connect the observed moisture to a possible entry point and make clear what remains uncertain. Share the history of the problem and any previous repairs so those details can inform the discussion.

What the evaluation can include

The scope may include an assessment, water intrusion testing, building envelope testing, moisture mapping or scheduled inspections. Which methods make sense depends on the building and the question being investigated.

Key facts to keep in mind:
- Coverage: Exterior systems that may include roofs, balconies, stairs, stucco, drainage and waterproofing.
- Timing: Scheduling and evaluation time depend on access, the affected area and the testing scope. Discuss timing when arranging an assessment.
- Cost factors: The size and location of the concern, access, testing needs and any repair work can affect the overall scope and cost. Request a clear explanation of what is included.

A few terms you may hear: water intrusion testing means checking for how water enters; moisture mapping records where moisture is present; building envelope refers to exterior components that separate indoor space from the outdoors. These terms describe different parts of investigating a leak, not a promise that one test will answer every question.

Leak concerns in Los Angeles properties

Exterior Systems Construction serves clients across Los Angeles, including single-family homes, multi-unit buildings and managed commercial properties. The appropriate investigation can differ between a home, an HOA property and a commercial building because the affected exterior systems and access needs may not be the same.

For a Los Angeles property, be ready to identify where the moisture appears, when it was noticed and whether anyone has tried repairs before. Those details help shape a practical testing scope. The best water leak detection plan is specific to the building and the reported issue, rather than a standard list of tests applied to every property.
