A local evaluation starts with the building

Local water leak detection is about investigating a particular moisture concern at the property, not assuming that every leak has the same cause. A mark inside may be related to an exterior system, but the source needs to be assessed. Exterior Systems Construction begins with an assessment and water intrusion testing to understand where moisture may be entering.

This is useful for homeowners, HOAs, property managers and commercial clients who need to plan their next step. Share what you have noticed, where it appears and whether earlier work addressed the same area. That history can help the team discuss the appropriate evaluation or testing scope.

What local leak detection may involve

Depending on the property and concern, an evaluation may use water intrusion testing, building envelope testing or moisture mapping. Scheduled inspections can also support repair planning for single-family homes, multi-unit buildings and managed properties.

Key facts for planning:
- What it covers: Investigation of possible moisture entry through exterior systems, with related repair options considered after assessment.
- Typical timing: The schedule depends on access, the affected location and how much testing is appropriate. Ask about timing when you discuss the scope.
- What affects cost: The reported area, building conditions, access, selected testing and the extent of any repair work all influence the work involved. The evaluation helps define that scope.

You may hear building envelope, meaning the exterior components that separate a building’s inside from outside conditions. Moisture mapping documents where moisture is found, while water intrusion testing helps investigate possible entry. Ask the provider to explain how each method relates to your specific concern.
